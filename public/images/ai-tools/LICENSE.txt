Every SVG in this folder comes from LobeHub Icons
(@lobehub/icons-static-svg 1.95.1, https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons).
The shapes are unchanged; only the size attributes, and for the white
files the fill colour, were edited.

- claude-color.svg, gemini-color.svg, perplexity-color.svg: LobeHub's colour
  versions, colours as published.
- openai.svg, grok.svg: monochrome marks, recoloured white for dark
  backgrounds.
- claude.svg, gemini.svg: earlier white versions, no longer used on the site.

The marks themselves are trademarks of Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Perplexity
AI and xAI respectively.
